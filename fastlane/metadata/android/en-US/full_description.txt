LetterBox is a game for young children who are learning their letters. It shows a picture, usually an animal, and three letters. Tap the letter the word starts with. Tap the picture to hear its name.

After a wrong answer, the same picture comes back so your child can try again. There are no scores, timers, or penalties.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Nine languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese (hiragana), and Korean (hangul). The language can be changed in the menu, independently of the system language.</li>
<li>About 70 pictures, with several pictures for many letters, so rounds stay varied.</li>
<li>Practise a selection of letters: all of them, the ten most common, or just one.</li>
<li>Optional progress tracking: letters your child knows well come up less often. Progress fades gently after a few weeks without practice, so forgotten letters return.</li>
<li>Works with a physical keyboard: press 1, 2, or 3, or the letter itself.</li>
<li>The menu button can be hidden until the app is closed, so small hands do not change settings by accident. Press and hold the top-right corner to bring it back.</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
LetterBox requests no permissions and has no internet access. There are no ads and no tracking. Progress is stored only on the device and is excluded from cloud backups.
